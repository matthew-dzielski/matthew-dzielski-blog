A little about me

Who you’ll be working with

I’m Matt Dzielski, a Realtor with Banga Realty in Northwest Indiana. I got into this because I love helping people — working hands-on with folks and putting sweat equity into homes, including my own.

I write this blog the way I work: plainspoken, practical, and focused on the decisions in front of you. Whether you’re buying your first place or selling the one you raised a family in, I’ll give you straight answers and steady guidance. I live and work in the Region, so when I talk about a neighborhood, I’ve probably driven it this week.

  1. 01
    Always a phone call away. I answer my own phone and return every message. When you have a question at 9pm about tomorrow’s showing, you’ll hear back from me — not a call center.
  2. 02
    Indiana-only focus. I work exclusively in Indiana. If a move takes you across the state line, I’ll help you find the right agent there — and I won’t pretend to know a market I don’t.
