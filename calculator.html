Indiana’s 1% homestead cap

Indiana caps property tax on your primary home at 1% of assessed value. The 0.85% default above reflects what most NWI homeowners actually pay after deductions — adjust it if you know your township’s rate.

NWI numbers, not national averages

Defaults start at a $277,000 home and a 7.3% rate — roughly today’s Northwest Indiana market. Plug in the actual list price of a house you’re eyeing and see what it really costs per month.
